# Usability test — five citizens, fifteen minutes each

Premium is judged by a first-time visitor on a phone, not by us. Run this before any further polish.

## Who

Five people who are not in tech and did not help build Politica. Aim for: two Telugu-first readers, one
Hindi-first, two English-first; at least two over 45; at least two who mostly use WhatsApp rather than
browsers. Pay for their time (a ₹500 voucher is fine).

## Setup

- Their own phone, their own network. Send the link on WhatsApp so the preview card is part of the test.
- Do not explain the site. Say: “Someone forwarded this to you. Use it the way you normally would.”
- One person runs the session, one takes notes. Record the screen if they agree.
- Ask them to think aloud. Never answer a question with a hint; say “what would you try?”

## Tasks (in this order)

1. **Find your own MLA.** Success: profile open within 60 seconds without help. Note what they typed or tapped first.
2. **Tell me one thing this person promised and whether it happened.** Success: they read a status and can say what “Unable to verify” means in their own words.
3. **Show me where that information came from.** Success: they tap a source link and understand it opens a news site.
4. **Switch the site to your language.** Success: they find the EN/TE/HI control unprompted.
5. **Share this promise with a friend.** Success: they use Share and a card appears in WhatsApp.
6. **React to this promise.** Success: they understand likes are opinions and (if live) that signing in needs only a phone number.
7. **Save the site to your home screen.** Success without help on Android; on iPhone note whether they find Share → Add to Home Screen.

## Questions after the tasks

- What is this site for, in one sentence?
- Does it favour any party? Why do you think so?
- Was anything slow, confusing, or felt untrustworthy?
- Would you open it again? When?

## What to record

For each task: success / with help / failed, time, the first thing they tapped, any words they said that
revealed a wrong model (“is this the government’s site?”, “is the number of cases the score?”).

## What counts as a problem worth fixing

Any task failed or needing help by **two or more** of the five. Fix those first; log the rest.

## Known risks to watch for

- “Unable to verify” read as “they did nothing”.
- Declared cases read as a score or a conviction.
- The leader strip read as a ranking.
- Language switch not noticed in the header on small phones.
- Search placeholder not understood as the main action.
